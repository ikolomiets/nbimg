# Gaspar Noe

- **Essence:** Extreme subjectivity designed to feel intoxicating, panicked, ecstatic, or physically disorienting.
- **Composition:** Overhead bodies and rooms, vertiginous symmetry, tunnel-like depth, unstable orientation.
- **Perspective:** Ultra-wide proximity, floating or spinning viewpoint translated into warped spatial tension.
- **Lighting:** Neon floods, strobes implied through alternating color zones, deep black voids.
- **Palette:** Acid red, magenta, violet, cyan, sodium orange.
- **Motion treatment:** Rotational blur, light trails, doubled edges, suspended bodies, spatial smear.
- **Atmosphere:** Hallucinatory, oppressive, bodily.
- **Anti-cues:** Avoid calm tasteful neon, clean commercial polish, comfortable eye-level balance, or effects that obscure required identity.
- **Films:** Enter the Void; Climax; Irreversible; Love; Lux Aeterna.
