# George Miller

## Essence

Miller tells stories through bodies, machines, faces, and landscapes in motion. However dense the action becomes, the viewer understands what matters and where to look. Strong silhouettes, centered points of attention, changing scale, and sharply connected gestures turn movement into visual music. His worlds are heightened and mythic, yet their materials, effort, danger, and humor remain palpable.

## Using the style flexibly

Give the scene a clear visual objective and organize every movement around it. This can energize a chase, dance, conversation, animal scene, workplace, or quiet moment of anticipation. Use bold shapes and expressive reactions while keeping geography and cause-and-effect legible. The style can be colorful, gentle, grotesque, or austere. Avoid assuming it requires vehicles, desert wastelands, rapid cutting, or orange-and-teal spectacle.

**Representative works:** *Mad Max 2*; *Mad Max: Fury Road*; *Furiosa: A Mad Max Saga*; *Babe: Pig in the City*; *Three Thousand Years of Longing*.
