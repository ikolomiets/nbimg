# Wong Kar-wai

- **Essence:** Urban time and space fracture around loneliness, memory, chance, and unfulfilled desire.
- **Composition:** Doorways, mirrors, windows, narrow corridors, obstructed foregrounds, intimate off-center framing.
- **Perspective:** Wide close proximity or telephoto voyeurism through layered foregrounds.
- **Lighting:** Mixed neon, green fluorescent spill, red practicals, wet reflections, smoke and deep shadow.
- **Palette:** Saturated red, green, cyan, amber, and dirty black.
- **Motion treatment:** Step-printed feeling translated as smeared crowds around a relatively still subject, directional blur, or suspended slow gesture.
- **Design and texture:** Cramped rooms, tiled passages, rain-slick streets, reflective glass, analog grain.
- **Atmosphere:** Humid, nocturnal, romantic, fragmented.
- **Modes:** Choose frenetic handheld blur or elegant claustrophobic stillness.
- **Anti-cues:** Avoid clean corporate neon, evenly lit faces, empty wide vistas, or combining frantic blur with pristine frozen action.
- **Films:** In the Mood for Love; Chungking Express; Fallen Angels; Happy Together; 2046.
