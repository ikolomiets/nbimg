# Wong Kar-wai

## Essence

Wong turns places into memories while people are still moving through them. Doorways, mirrors, windows, passing crowds, cramped rooms, music-implied rhythm, and changing light make intimacy feel both immediate and unreachable. Time may seem smeared, suspended, repeated, or compressed, and the city becomes a network of missed connections, private rituals, and sensual fragments.

## Using the style flexibly

Use obstruction, reflection, movement, color, or shallow moments of attention to express the emotional distance already present in the scene. Daylight, countryside, open space, calm stillness, or muted color can work as well as neon night if the image retains subjective memory and longing. Avoid automatically adding rain, cigarettes, green and red neon, motion-blurred crowds, narrow corridors, or 1960s Hong Kong décor.

**Representative works:** *Days of Being Wild*; *Chungking Express*; *Fallen Angels*; *In the Mood for Love*; *2046*.
