# Federico Fellini

- **Essence:** Memory, desire, guilt, and fame become surreal public spectacle.
- **Composition:** Processions, theatrical crowds, frontal pageantry, oversized faces, layered social tableaux.
- **Perspective:** Expansive stage-like wides with sudden intimate grotesque portraits.
- **Lighting:** Studio moonlight, theatrical spot pools, luminous fog, glamorous high contrast.
- **Palette:** Rich monochrome or faded carnival color, cream, black, red, gold.
- **Design and texture:** Circuses, artificial streets, Catholic ornament, decadent parties, costumes and masks.
- **Atmosphere:** Carnivalesque, sensual, melancholy, dream-autobiographical.
- **Anti-cues:** Avoid tidy realism, sparse minimalism, generic fantasy creatures, or crowds without visual hierarchy.
- **Films:** 8 1/2; La Dolce Vita; Amarcord; Nights of Cabiria; Satyricon.
