# Quentin Tarantino

- **Essence:** Retro genre history remixed into swaggering, theatrical pulp.
- **Composition:** Low angles, confrontational ensembles, graphic close-ups, staged standoffs, bold silhouettes.
- **Perspective:** Crisp genre framing with occasional exaggerated wide-angle emphasis.
- **Lighting:** Hard practical light, sun-baked exteriors, smoky interiors, stylized night color.
- **Palette:** Mustard, tobacco, blood red, black, cream, saturated costume accents.
- **Design and texture:** Period signage without copied logos, diners, cars, pulp interiors, tactile wardrobe.
- **Atmosphere:** Suspenseful, irreverent, explosive.
- **Anti-cues:** Avoid copying recognizable scenes, costumes, characters, typography, or treating violence as the only style cue.
- **Films:** Pulp Fiction; Kill Bill; Reservoir Dogs; Inglourious Basterds; Django Unchained.
