# David Cronenberg

## Essence

Cronenberg makes ideas about identity, desire, disease, technology, and control visible through the body and the objects surrounding it. His worlds often look calm, intelligent, and clinically ordered even when something impossible or disturbing is emerging. Flesh, machines, interiors, clothing, and tools share a tactile reality, making transformation feel intimate rather than merely spectacular.

## Using the style flexibly

Begin with a credible material world and allow one physical detail or relationship to expose a deeper psychological unease. The scene need not contain mutation or gore; posture, symmetry, a polished surface, a medical object, or an ambiguous interface can suggest the same tension between mind and matter. Keep the tone curious as well as unsettling. Avoid generic body horror, random organic machinery, or grotesque effects detached from the subject's emotional or conceptual conflict.

**Representative works:** *Videodrome*; *The Fly*; *Dead Ringers*; *Crash*; *A History of Violence*.
