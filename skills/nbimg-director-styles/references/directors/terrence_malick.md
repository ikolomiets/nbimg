# Terrence Malick

- **Essence:** Nature becomes a spiritual field of memory, grace, violence, and loss.
- **Composition:** Low drifting viewpoint through grass or water, faces turned toward light, bodies integrated with landscape.
- **Perspective:** Intimate wide-angle proximity, expansive sky, layered natural depth.
- **Lighting:** Golden hour, backlit foliage, dappled sun, luminous cloud, natural flare.
- **Palette:** Wheat gold, leaf green, river blue, warm skin, soft earth.
- **Motion treatment:** Wind through fabric and grass, running bodies, water spray, fleeting gestures.
- **Atmosphere:** Lyrical, searching, reverent, transient.
- **Anti-cues:** Avoid studio polish, rigid symmetry, synthetic landscapes, heavy artificial color, or frozen posed figures.
- **Films:** The Tree of Life; Days of Heaven; The Thin Red Line; The New World; A Hidden Life.
