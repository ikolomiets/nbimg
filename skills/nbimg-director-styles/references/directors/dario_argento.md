# Dario Argento

- **Essence:** Giallo dread becomes an operatic collision of color, architecture, voyeurism, and stylized danger.
- **Composition:** Oversized baroque rooms, extreme eye close-ups, stalking POV geometry, figures dwarfed by decor.
- **Perspective:** Wide-angle gliding menace, high or impossible vantage, graphic close detail.
- **Lighting:** Extreme chiaroscuro, saturated crimson and cobalt keys, sharp steel-blue beams through darkness.
- **Palette:** Violent primary clashes: red, cobalt, green, black; or bright white daylight punctured by red.
- **Design and texture:** Velvet, patterned walls, glass, modernist or baroque interiors, polished blades only when relevant.
- **Motion treatment:** Sweeping directional tension, moving shadow patterns, a frozen instant of discovery.
- **Atmosphere:** Theatrical, dreamlike, voyeuristic, threatening.
- **Modes:** Choose saturated fairy-tale darkness or bright modern daylight dread.
- **Anti-cues:** Avoid muddy color, naturalistic illumination, generic slasher imagery, or copied scenes and murder set pieces.
- **Films:** Suspiria; Deep Red; Tenebre; Phenomena; The Bird with the Crystal Plumage.
