# Guillermo del Toro

- **Essence:** Wounded beauty and creature empathy inside tactile gothic history.
- **Composition:** Ornate depth, arches and windows as frames, close figures against elaborate machinery.
- **Perspective:** Wide environmental intimacy without grotesque facial distortion; organic depth falloff.
- **Lighting:** Steel-blue or pale green side light opposed by warm amber practicals, fireplaces, candles, and volumetric haze.
- **Palette:** Cyan-amber contrast, blood red, aged gold, oxidized green, rich black.
- **Design and texture:** Gears, insects, old books, religious objects, wet stone, velvet, scarred organic surfaces.
- **Atmosphere:** Romantic, ominous, compassionate, materially lush.
- **Modes:** Choose fairy-tale ruin, gothic-industrial laboratory, or haunted aristocratic interior.
- **Anti-cues:** Avoid sterile digital surfaces, generic monsters, flat lighting, or copied creature and set designs.
- **Films:** Pan's Labyrinth; The Shape of Water; Hellboy; Crimson Peak; Nightmare Alley.
