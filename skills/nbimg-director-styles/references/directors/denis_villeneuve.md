# Denis Villeneuve

- **Essence:** Human vulnerability measured against enormous systems, landscapes, machines, and architecture.
- **Composition:** Vast negative space, solitary small figures, monumental geometry, severe horizon lines.
- **Perspective:** Wide-angle environmental scale, slow floating vantage, occasional disorienting aerial geometry.
- **Lighting:** Soft naturalist single-source light, bright silhouette fields, deep reverse-angle shadow.
- **Palette:** Sand, slate, fog gray, pale white, muted ochre, restrained orange.
- **Design and texture:** Brutalist concrete, desert dust, monolithic forms, sparse advanced machinery.
- **Atmosphere:** Austere, quiet, foreboding, sublime.
- **Modes:** Choose desert monumentality, fogbound minimalism, or stark black-and-white trauma.
- **Anti-cues:** Avoid busy ornament, colorful cyberpunk clutter, casual framing, or scale without a human reference.
- **Films:** Dune; Dune: Part Two; Blade Runner 2049; Arrival; Sicario.
