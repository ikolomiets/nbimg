# Martin Scorsese

## Essence

Scorsese's images move with the energy of a mind remembering, desiring, judging, or losing control. Fluid observation can give way to sudden stillness, expressive color, harsh intimacy, or subjective distortion. Streets, homes, clubs, sacred spaces, and elaborate social worlds are alive with status and temptation. Visual bravura remains tied to a character's moral and emotional experience.

## Using the style flexibly

Choose whose experience drives the scene and let framing, movement, light, and surrounding detail share that perspective. The tone may be exuberant, devotional, anxious, elegant, comic, or violent; there is no single Scorsese palette or camera move. Create momentum through attention rather than adding spectacle indiscriminately. Avoid reducing the style to gangsters, tracking shots, red light, freeze frames, or copied urban nightlife.

**Representative works:** *Taxi Driver*; *Raging Bull*; *Goodfellas*; *The Age of Innocence*; *Silence*.
