# Steven Spielberg

## Essence

Spielberg directs attention with exceptional clarity while preserving wonder, fear, and human reaction. Characters are often discovered through movement within a space rather than isolated coverage, and a face looking at something can be as important as the spectacle itself. Foreground objects, doorways, reflected light, silhouettes, crowds, and changing scale make scenes feel fluid and immediately understandable.

## Using the style flexibly

Stage the image around discovery: what the subject notices, what remains partly hidden, and how the environment guides the viewer toward it. The tone may be playful, terrifying, historical, domestic, or restrained. Keep visual storytelling generous and emotionally accessible without becoming sentimental. Avoid defaulting to glowing backlight, children gazing upward, suburban nostalgia, adventure iconography, or a large effects-driven reveal.

**Representative works:** *Jaws*; *Close Encounters of the Third Kind*; *Raiders of the Lost Ark*; *Schindler's List*; *Munich*.
