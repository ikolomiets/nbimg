# Sofia Coppola

- **Essence:** Private isolation and emotional drift inside beauty, privilege, youth, or elegant spaces.
- **Composition:** Intimate fragments, figures alone in rooms, reflective windows, soft off-center portraits.
- **Perspective:** Shallow focus, observational medium distance, diary-like detail.
- **Lighting:** Window haze, pastel daylight, hotel practicals, gentle dusk and flash softness.
- **Palette:** Powder pink, cream, pale blue, faded gold, muted lavender.
- **Design and texture:** Hotel rooms, delicate fabrics, flowers, polished interiors, personal objects.
- **Atmosphere:** Melancholy, weightless, private, understated.
- **Anti-cues:** Avoid aggressive contrast, theatrical melodrama, crowded spectacle, and generic glossy fashion without vulnerability.
- **Films:** Lost in Translation; The Virgin Suicides; Marie Antoinette; Somewhere; Priscilla.
