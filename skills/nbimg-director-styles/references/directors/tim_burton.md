# Tim Burton

- **Essence:** Emotionally sincere outsiders inhabit childlike yet macabre expressionist worlds.
- **Composition:** Crooked silhouettes, exaggerated verticals, spirals, twisted trees, theatrical staging.
- **Perspective:** Slight wide-angle exaggeration and graphic cutout depth.
- **Lighting:** Moonlit blue shadows, stark expressionist highlights, warm pockets inside gothic darkness.
- **Palette:** Charcoal, bone white, desaturated blue, bruised purple, selective red or amber.
- **Design and texture:** Stripes, graveyards, crooked roofs, handmade props, pale figures, ornate decay.
- **Atmosphere:** Gothic whimsy, loneliness, playful morbidity.
- **Anti-cues:** Avoid photoreal gore, sleek modern minimalism, naturalistic suburbia, or exact character designs.
- **Films:** Edward Scissorhands; Beetlejuice; Batman Returns; Sleepy Hollow; Corpse Bride.
