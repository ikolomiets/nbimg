# Tim Burton

## Essence

Burton gives outsider emotion a handmade visual world. Architecture, silhouettes, costume, landscape, and exaggerated proportions bend toward loneliness, curiosity, romance, and macabre humor. Artificiality is part of the sincerity: theatrical sets and graphic shapes can express feelings more directly than realism, while small pockets of warmth protect vulnerable characters from the worlds around them.

## Using the style flexibly

Let the setting reflect how the subject feels different from their surroundings. This may use crooked or elongated forms, but it can also work through color contrast, scale, negative space, or an unexpectedly tender object. The scene need not be gothic, nocturnal, or fantastical. Avoid copying striped costumes, pale faces, spiral hills, graveyards, scissors, or familiar character designs; preserve emotional oddness rather than merchandise-like iconography.

**Representative works:** *Beetlejuice*; *Edward Scissorhands*; *Batman Returns*; *Ed Wood*; *Sleepy Hollow*.
