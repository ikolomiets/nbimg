# Zhang Yimou

## Essence

Zhang uses color, landscape, architecture, fabric, weather, and groups of bodies to turn private feeling into a public image. Repetition and ceremony can reveal social power, while a single person or contrasting color breaks the pattern with great emotional force. His work ranges from intimate rural realism to monumental spectacle, joined by a painterly sensitivity to human figures within organized space.

## Using the style flexibly

Choose one visual relationship that expresses the scene—individual against group, color against environment, movement against stillness, or desire against ritual—and develop it clearly. Scale can be modest or immense, and the palette should grow from the subject rather than imitate a famous film. Avoid filling every frame with red fabric, lanterns, martial formations, falling leaves, or ornate historical costumes.

**Representative works:** *Raise the Red Lantern*; *Ju Dou*; *To Live*; *Hero*; *Shadow*.
