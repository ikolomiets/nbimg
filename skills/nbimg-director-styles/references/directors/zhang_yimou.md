# Zhang Yimou

- **Essence:** Emotion and power organized through ceremonial color, landscape, repetition, and choreographed bodies.
- **Composition:** Geometric formations, repeated costumes or objects, painterly wides, strong architectural axes.
- **Perspective:** Expansive landscapes and elevated formal tableaux with precise human patterns.
- **Lighting:** Luminous natural landscapes, controlled interior glow, weather used graphically.
- **Palette:** Commit to one dominant symbolic color family with restrained complements: red, gold, green, blue, or white.
- **Design and texture:** Silk, banners, lanterns, courtyards, seasonal nature, disciplined period detail.
- **Motion treatment:** Fabric, arrows, leaves, rain, or bodies forming coherent directional patterns.
- **Atmosphere:** Ceremonial, tragic, ravishing, disciplined.
- **Anti-cues:** Avoid mixing every signature color, random spectacle, unreadable crowds, or copied costumes and sets.
- **Films:** Raise the Red Lantern; Hero; House of Flying Daggers; Ju Dou; Shadow.
