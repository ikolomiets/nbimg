# Park Chan-wook

## Essence

Park arranges desire, secrecy, cruelty, and tenderness with sensuous precision. Rooms, objects, clothing, weather, and bodies seem to participate in elaborate visual rhymes. A polished surface may conceal violence; an elegant composition may shift meaning when a hidden relationship is revealed. The camera's intelligence feels playful and exact without making the characters emotionally remote.

## Using the style flexibly

Find visual correspondences within the requested scene: shapes, gestures, textures, partitions, or viewpoints that connect characters and suggest concealed knowledge. Rich design can help, but a plain room or landscape works if its details are purposeful. Let beauty complicate the moral tone rather than simply glamorize it. Avoid generic luxury advertising, gratuitous gore, copied corridor fights, or forcing symmetry onto every image.

**Representative works:** *Oldboy*; *Lady Vengeance*; *The Handmaiden*; *Decision to Leave*; *Thirst*.
