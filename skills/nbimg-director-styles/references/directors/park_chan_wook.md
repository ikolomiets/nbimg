# Park Chan-wook

- **Essence:** Beauty, revenge, desire, and corruption arranged with luxurious precision.
- **Composition:** Immaculate symmetry or deliberate lateral balance, mirrors, visual rhymes, concealed spatial reveals.
- **Perspective:** Controlled wide interiors, elegant profile framing, selective compressed observation.
- **Lighting:** Polished soft keys, jewel-toned shadow, controlled pools of practical light.
- **Palette:** Lacquer red, jade, cream, black, deep blue, rich wood.
- **Design and texture:** Refined interiors, silk, polished wood, glass, patterned wallpaper, precise props.
- **Atmosphere:** Sensual, tense, perverse, elegant.
- **Anti-cues:** Avoid messy handheld chaos, generic luxury advertising, gratuitous gore without compositional purpose, or copied sets.
- **Films:** Oldboy; The Handmaiden; Decision to Leave; Lady Vengeance; Thirst.
