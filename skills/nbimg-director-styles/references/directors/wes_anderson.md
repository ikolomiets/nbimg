# Wes Anderson

- **Essence:** Handmade storybook order, emotional restraint, and nostalgic precision.
- **Composition:** Centered symmetry, flat frontality, parallel lines, eye-level tableaux, occasional overhead object layouts.
- **Perspective:** Deep, theatrical staging with minimal distortion; lateral spatial organization.
- **Lighting:** Soft diffused light, gentle tungsten warmth, restrained shadows.
- **Palette:** Controlled 60:30:10 relationships; muted pastel field, supporting period color, one saturated accent.
- **Design and texture:** Miniature-like sets, labeled objects, patterned walls, tailored wardrobe, analog craft.
- **Atmosphere:** Deadpan, whimsical, wistful.
- **Modes:** Choose pastel hotel formality, warm childhood outdoors, or saturated family interiors.
- **Anti-cues:** Avoid casual asymmetry, harsh uncontrolled contrast, gritty handheld realism, or random rainbow palettes.
- **Films:** The Grand Budapest Hotel; Moonrise Kingdom; The Royal Tenenbaums; Fantastic Mr. Fox.
