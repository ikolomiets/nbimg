# Nicolas Winding Refn

- **Essence:** Myth, masculinity, beauty, and emptiness reduced to glossy neon ritual.
- **Composition:** Symmetrical poses, sparse negative space, frontal icons, reflective corridors.
- **Perspective:** Slow observational distance, fashion-editorial precision, selective close profiles.
- **Lighting:** Pink-blue neon, hard colored rims, deep black, glossy reflections.
- **Palette:** Magenta, cobalt, cyan, crimson, black.
- **Design and texture:** Polished cars, glass, satin, chrome, empty luxury spaces.
- **Atmosphere:** Hypnotic, cool, menacing, ceremonial.
- **Anti-cues:** Avoid dense cyberpunk clutter, comic-book action, uncontrolled handheld movement, or color without negative space.
- **Films:** Drive; Only God Forgives; The Neon Demon; Bronson; Valhalla Rising.
