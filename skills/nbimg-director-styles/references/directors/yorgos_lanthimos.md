# Yorgos Lanthimos

- **Essence:** Social rituals look alien, clinical, and faintly monstrous.
- **Composition:** Stiff blocking, symmetrical interiors, excessive headroom, emotionally distant group arrangements.
- **Perspective:** Wide-angle or fisheye distortion, deep focus, surveillance-like distance.
- **Lighting:** Cool available light, sterile brightness, restrained period softness where appropriate.
- **Palette:** Institutional beige, pale blue, sickly green, muted burgundy, porcelain white.
- **Design and texture:** Formal rooms, rigid furniture, ceremonial costume, uncanny cleanliness.
- **Atmosphere:** Deadpan, absurd, detached, uncomfortable.
- **Anti-cues:** Avoid expressive glamour posing, warm sentimental intimacy, conventional flattering portraiture, and random surreal props.
- **Films:** The Favourite; Poor Things; The Lobster; Dogtooth; Kinds of Kindness.
