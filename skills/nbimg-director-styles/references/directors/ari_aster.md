# Ari Aster

- **Essence:** Grief and emotional collapse become visible through houses, families, ceremonies, and inescapable spatial design.
- **Composition:** Static symmetrical wides, dollhouse-like interiors, ritual circles, tiny figures in controlled spaces.
- **Perspective:** Distant deep-focus observation, slow-zoom tension translated as compressed visual scrutiny.
- **Lighting:** Bright daylight horror, warm domestic practicals, darkness held just beyond readable rooms.
- **Palette:** Pale pastoral color, domestic brown, floral white, restrained red, deep night black.
- **Design and texture:** Family homes, ceremonial textiles, miniatures, flowers, geometric communal spaces.
- **Atmosphere:** Controlled, anxious, mournful, inevitable.
- **Anti-cues:** Avoid jump-scare staging, chaotic handheld imagery, generic gore, or daylight treated as cheerful.
- **Films:** Hereditary; Midsommar; Beau Is Afraid.
