# Baz Luhrmann

- **Essence:** Romance, tragedy, fame, and melodrama become maximalist pop-operatic spectacle.
- **Composition:** Sweeping diagonals, theatrical centerpieces, layered crowds, rapid-montage energy condensed into one frame.
- **Perspective:** Grand wide spectacle with flamboyant close foreground accents.
- **Lighting:** Footlights, spotlights, glittering practicals, fireworks, saturated backlight.
- **Palette:** Jewel red, gold, electric blue, black, glittering white.
- **Design and texture:** Ornate sets, sequins, decorative typography only when explicitly requested, flowers, chandeliers, theatrical costume.
- **Motion treatment:** Fabric, confetti, dancers, and camera-like sweep frozen at a peak instant.
- **Atmosphere:** Exuberant, romantic, tragic, overwhelming.
- **Anti-cues:** Avoid restrained minimalism, empty sets, muddy color, or copied costumes and production designs.
- **Films:** Moulin Rouge!; Romeo + Juliet; The Great Gatsby; Elvis; Strictly Ballroom.
