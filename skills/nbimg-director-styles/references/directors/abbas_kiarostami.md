# Abbas Kiarostami

## Essence

Kiarostami's images find mystery in ordinary life. Roads, hills, windows, car interiors, faces, and small human gestures are observed with patient clarity, allowing landscape and everyday behavior to carry philosophical weight. The frame often feels simple at first and gradually reveals uncertainty: documentary and fiction overlap, distance becomes intimacy, and an unassuming place begins to suggest a larger moral or spiritual question.

## Using the style flexibly

Keep the scene open, humane, and attentive rather than heavily designed. Let natural surroundings, modest architecture, or the boundary of a window organize the image. A quiet portrait, crowded street, interior, or fantastical setting can all work if the world feels genuinely observed and leaves room for the viewer to discover meaning. Avoid treating the style as only rural Iran, winding roads, or minimalist emptiness.

**Representative works:** *Close-Up*; *Taste of Cherry*; *Where Is the Friend's House?*; *The Wind Will Carry Us*; *Certified Copy*.
