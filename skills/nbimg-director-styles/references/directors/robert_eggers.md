# Robert Eggers

- **Essence:** The historical past feels materially exact, spiritually dangerous, and culturally alien.
- **Composition:** Ritual symmetry, severe profiles, constrained period framing, figures dominated by landscape or architecture.
- **Perspective:** Formal wide tableaux and close tactile details without modern visual polish.
- **Lighting:** Candlelight, firelight, overcast sky, fog, hard coastal daylight.
- **Palette:** Soot black, bone, mud brown, cold gray, dried blood, weathered wood.
- **Design and texture:** Period-accurate tools, rough cloth, timber, stone, mud, salt, smoke.
- **Atmosphere:** Archaic, superstitious, oppressive, austere.
- **Anti-cues:** Avoid clean fantasy costumes, modern grooming, glossy horror lighting, anachronistic objects, or generic medieval imagery.
- **Films:** The Witch; The Lighthouse; The Northman; Nosferatu.
