# Hayao Miyazaki

- **Essence:** Ecological attention, flight, labor, childhood, and gentle wonder in richly inhabited hand-drawn worlds.
- **Composition:** Expansive skies, layered landscapes, small figures in nature, readable domestic spaces, dynamic flight paths.
- **Perspective:** Clear illustrative depth with atmospheric distance and inviting environmental detail.
- **Lighting:** Luminous daylight, glowing sunsets, soft cloud shadow, warm domestic light.
- **Palette:** Sky blue, leaf green, cloud white, warm earth, selective magical accents.
- **Design and texture:** Hand-drawn linework, painted backgrounds, plants, food, workshops, whimsical machinery.
- **Motion treatment:** Wind in grass and hair, drifting clouds, flight, water, purposeful everyday gestures.
- **Atmosphere:** Humane, adventurous, contemplative, wondrous.
- **Anti-cues:** Avoid copying recognizable characters or creatures, generic anime gloss, sterile 3D surfaces, or spectacle without everyday life.
- **Films:** My Neighbor Totoro; Princess Mononoke; Spirited Away; Howl's Moving Castle; Nausicaa of the Valley of the Wind.
