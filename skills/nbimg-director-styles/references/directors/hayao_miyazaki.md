# Hayao Miyazaki

## Essence

Miyazaki's images combine wonder with close attention to how life works. Wind, clouds, water, plants, food, tools, buildings, and machines feel animated by use and care. Expansive adventures pause for ordinary labor or quiet observation, while fantastical beings belong to an ecological and emotional world rather than appearing as isolated spectacle. Movement is graceful, purposeful, and full of changing weather.

## Using the style flexibly

Make the scene inviting to explore and give both foreground action and distant environment meaningful life. A realistic city, interior, industrial site, or adult subject can work as well as childhood fantasy if everyday activity and natural forces remain present. Balance awe with calm, detail with clarity, and danger with humane curiosity. Avoid copying recognizable characters, creatures, aircraft, costumes, or reducing the look to generic anime softness.

**Representative works:** *My Neighbor Totoro*; *Princess Mononoke*; *Spirited Away*; *Nausicaä of the Valley of the Wind*; *The Wind Rises*.
